About Insurance Careers Hub

Insurance Careers Hub helps job seekers find real, open roles at local insurance agencies: sales producers, customer service reps, account managers and more. You don't need to be licensed to start. Many agencies will train the right person and help you get your Property & Casualty or Life & Health license.

  • Real openings: listings refresh daily and closed jobs drop off.
  • Pay up front: we show pay whenever the agency publishes it.
  • Apply in minutes: each Apply button takes you to the agency's application on Staff My Agency.

Jobs listed here are with independent insurance agencies and are posted through Staff My Agency. Applications are handled on Staff My Agency. Insurance Careers Hub is not the employer, and agency staff are employed by the agency owner, not by the insurance carrier.
